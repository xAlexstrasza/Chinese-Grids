# Chinese Grids

Printable Chinese character practice sheets, made right in the browser.

**Open it:** https://xalexstrasza.github.io/Chinese-Grids/

## Three kinds of sheets

- **Characters** – each character (or word) gets its own row: a model to copy, gray copies to trace, then empty boxes. Pinyin, step-by-step stroke order and the meaning sit above the row.
- **Text** – paste a text or a poem and it flows through the grid box by box, with pinyin on top. Add tracing rows or empty rows under each line.
- **Questions** – numbered questions, each followed by empty rows for the answer.

## Also

- 米 rice, 田 field, 九宫 nine-square or plain grids, in any color
- Box size, row and column gaps, margins, A4 or Letter paper
- A title, a name and date line, and page numbers on every page
- Tone-colored pinyin, and dictation sheets that hide the characters but keep the pinyin
- Word lists with your own pinyin and meanings, one per line: `朋友 péngyou friend` (numbers work too: `lv4se4`)
- Tap any character to watch its stroke order, then practice writing it with your mouse or finger
- Share a worksheet as a link
- Print it, or pick **Save as PDF** in the print dialog

Everything runs in the browser. Your text and settings are saved in this browser's local storage.

## Run it on your computer

It's a single static page: open `index.html`, or serve the folder, for example with `npx serve .`
